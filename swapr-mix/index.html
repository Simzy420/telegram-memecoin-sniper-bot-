How to Use

Use Swapr Mix for ordinary motions such as dancing or walking. Mark a cup or a phone when that object should stay in the clip.

How Mix works

  1. Choose a motion. Swipe sideways. Clips autoplay muted. Tap one to select it. Nothing is generated yet. Refresh reloads the gallery.
  2. Mark person (green). Tap the body or face of the person to replace.
  3. Mark keep (red). Tap a cup, phone, or other object that should stay from the template. Clear marks removes every dot. With no red marks, Generate asks you to confirm, because unmarked props may disappear.
  4. Upload a still of yourself (JPG or PNG). A face or full body works best. This step stays locked until a motion is selected. You can also drop a photo on the upload area.
  5. The prompt is optional. It already asks to replace only the person and keep handheld objects from the template.
  6. Tap Generate Mix. It stays off until a motion is selected, a still is uploaded, and at least one green person mark is placed.

Waiting while it starts

The first Generate may take several minutes while the Runpod worker starts. The status line shows when the job is queued or running. Leave this tab open. If it times out, wait a minute and try once.

The page shows the Runpod balance. Stop GPU stops the Runpod worker.

Result

When a clip is ready, Result shows the video with playback controls. Download saves the file. Save to Photos opens the phone share sheet when this browser can share a video file, and downloads the file otherwise.

Extend

After a result, Extend for a longer clip offers a target length from 8 to 22 seconds, plus Extend once and Auto-extend. Extend still uses the Hugging Face Space and cannot continue a Runpod clip. Generate on this page runs on Runpod, so those Extend buttons will not lengthen that result.

Templates

Motions come from the same catalog as Swapr: Simzy/wan22-template-clips.
