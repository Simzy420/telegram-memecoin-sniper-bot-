How Mix works

Why this exists

Original Swapr (Become the Character) is unchanged. Mix is a separate app that sends SAM control points so you can choose which people and which props Wan Animate replaces.

Use Mix

  1. Pick a motion from the gallery.
  2. Mark person — tap green once on each person to replace. Person 1, Person 2, and so on match the numbers on the dots.
  3. Mark prop — tap red once on each prop to replace (popsicle, cup, phone). Leave this out if you only want people replaced.
  4. Upload a face per person — fill every Person slot, and every Prop slot you marked.
  5. Tap Generate Mix. Leave the tab open while Runpod runs.
