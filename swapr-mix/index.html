How Mix works

Why this exists

Original Swapr (Become the Character) is unchanged. Mix is a separate app that sends SAM control points so Wan Animate replaces the person and can leave handheld props alone when you mark them.

Use Mix

  1. Pick a motion from the gallery.
  2. Mark person — tap green on the body/face to replace.
  3. Mark keep — tap red on props (popsicle, cup, phone) that must stay from the template.
  4. Upload your still of the person (a still that includes the prop helps even more).
  5. Tap Generate Mix. Leave the tab open while Runpod runs.
